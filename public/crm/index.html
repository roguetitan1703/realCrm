The problem

Most firms run on four inboxes and a notebook.

Leads are everywhere

Enquiries come from 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, WhatsApp, calls and walk-ins. They end up in portal dashboards, phones and spreadsheets, and some are never called.

Follow-ups depend on memory

A promised callback lives in an agent's head or diary. When it is missed, nobody finds out until the buyer has moved on.

The owner can't see the work

Who called whom today, what came of it, and which leads nobody has touched: the answers take a round of phone calls to the team.

What it does

Built around how a property firm actually works.

Leads and follow-ups

  • Portal and website enquiries become leads automatically and are assigned to agents in turn.
  • Each lead has a status, a next follow-up and a full history of calls, WhatsApp messages, remarks and visits.
  • Ready-made lists: not contacted, going cold, follow-up overdue, and buyers who enquired again.
  • A repeat enquiry from the same number is added to the existing lead, not created twice.
  • Rejected leads keep the reason, such as budget or locality mismatch.
  • Import existing leads from Excel or CSV, checked before anything is saved, with undo.

Calling owners

  • A calling list of property owners, grouped by project and tower.
  • Call outcomes such as call not received, switched off, interested, key received and do not call.
  • Callbacks are scheduled and show up on the agent's list when due.
  • Assign a whole project to an agent in one step.
  • When an owner hands over the key, convert the row into a listing.

Listings

  • Flats, offices, shops and warehouses, for sale or rent, grouped by project, tower and unit.
  • Photos and videos, with your firm's logo and name added to each photo.
  • One link per listing to send on WhatsApp. It shows the photos only: no price, flat number or owner.
  • Each lead shows the listings that match what they asked for, ready to share.
  • WhatsApp messages written for you, in English or Marathi.

Site visits and deals

  • Site visits are logged with a selfie and the phone's location.
  • A calendar of visits and tasks, with reminders before each visit.
  • Close a deal as a rent or sale agreement, with the documents attached.
  • Tenants and buyers are kept as contacts, alongside owners.
  • A reminder 30 days before a rent agreement ends.

Team and visibility

  • Owner, manager and agent roles. Agents see only their own leads.
  • A dashboard of what needs attention today: uncontacted leads, overdue follow-ups, callbacks due.
  • Each agent's calls, pickups and status changes, for today, 7 or 30 days.
  • Missed follow-ups and uncalled leads, counted per agent.
  • Move an agent's work to someone else when they are on leave or leave the firm.

On the phone

  • Installs on Android and iPhone from the browser. No app store needed.
  • The agent's day as one list: overdue, due today, then new leads.
  • Call from the app, then record the outcome and the next step.
  • Notifications for a new lead, a follow-up due, a visit coming up or a buyer enquiring again.

Your website

Excel and CSV

Bring in existing leads, owners and listings, with a report of any row that was not imported and why.

WhatsApp and calls

Messages open in WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business on the agent's phone with the text ready. Calls go through the phone's own dialler.
