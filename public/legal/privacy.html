This policy explains how we handle personal data in the Service. It applies to (a) our customers, the real estate businesses and their team members who use the Service (“Clients”), and (b) people who contact those Clients, such as property buyers, sellers and tenants (“Contacts”).

1. Our role

For Contacts’ data, the Client decides why and how it is used, and Delpat processes it on the Client’s behalf to provide the Service. For Clients’ own account data, Delpat is responsible for it.

2. Data we process

  • Client account data: names, email addresses, phone numbers, company details, login and usage information.
  • Lead and CRM data entered or imported by Clients, including from property portals: names, phone numbers, email addresses, property requirements, notes and activity history.
  • Data from connected messaging accounts. When a Client connects their WhatsApp Business account or Instagram professional account through Meta’s official platforms (the WhatsApp Business Platform / Cloud API and the Instagram API), we process:
    • messages sent and received through the connected account, including text, images, documents, voice notes and other media;
    • phone numbers, WhatsApp profile names, Instagram usernames and profile names of people who message the Client;
    • message metadata such as timestamps and delivery and read status;
    • account identifiers and access tokens issued by Meta to operate the connection.

3. How we use data

Only to provide and support the Service: showing conversations in the Client’s inbox, creating and updating leads, assigning conversations to the Client’s team members, sending messages the Client chooses to send, reminders and reporting for the Client, security, troubleshooting and legal compliance.

4. Messages from non-leads

A connected WhatsApp or Instagram account may receive messages from people who are not property enquiries. Messages from numbers or accounts that the Client has not saved as leads are held only briefly so the Client can choose to save them as a lead, and are then automatically deleted. Contacts the Client marks as personal are ignored.

5. What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not use messaging data for advertising or to build profiles across Clients. We do not use Contacts’ data for our own marketing.

6. Sharing

We share data only with:

  • the Client who owns the account and their authorised team members;
  • service providers that host and run the Service (such as cloud hosting and email delivery), under confidentiality and security obligations;
  • Meta Platforms, as needed to send and receive WhatsApp and Instagram messages;
  • authorities, where required by law.

7. Retention

Data is kept while the Client’s account is active, or until the Client deletes it. When a Client disconnects a messaging account or ends their subscription, data obtained through that connection is deleted within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Records that a page of homes shared with you was opened are deleted after 180 days unless you have become that business’s contact. The link between the random identifier kept for your browser and you is deleted once it has gone unused for 180 days; the identifier itself stays on any records that are kept.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Access is limited to authorised personnel who need it to operate and support the Service.

9. Your rights

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data. If you contacted a business that uses our Service, you can ask that business directly or follow our Data Deletion page: https://realestate.delpat.in/data-deletion. We respond within 48 hours.
