Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 2026

Delpat LLP respects your right to control your personal data. This page explains how to request deletion of data held in Delpat Real Estate CRM, including data received through WhatsApp or Instagram when you contacted a business that uses our software.

How to request deletion

  1. Email info@delpat.in with the subject “Data Deletion Request”.
  2. Include the phone number, Instagram username or email address the data relates to, and the name of the business you contacted, if you know it.
  3. We may ask you to confirm the request from that phone number or account, to verify it is yours.

What we delete

All messages, contact details, lead records and media linked to your identifier in our systems. Backups are overwritten in their normal cycle.

Timeline

We confirm receipt within 48 hours and complete deletion within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email when it is done.

For businesses using our CRM

You can disconnect your WhatsApp or Instagram account at any time from Settings in the CRM. All data obtained through that connection is deleted within 30 days of disconnection.
