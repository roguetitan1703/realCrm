Call, WhatsApp, remark
Nothing moves. A booking stays until you say how it went.
Not received · busy · switched off
Status → Call Not Received. Next try booked tomorrow, same time, unless you pick another. The person leaves Overdue.
3rd miss in a row: Reject · Not Answering is offered. Never automatic.
Asked to call back · site visit
Needs a time. Booked at the time you pick; a push at that time (visit: an hour before).
Connected · sent details · interested
Status → Follow-Up (Interested stays Interested). Call tomorrow 11:00 unless you pick another or No next step.
Not interested · booked elsewhere · wrong number
Status → Rejected with the reason. The booking is cleared. Out of your queue.
A new next step
Replaces the open one. One booking per person.
Enquired again
Rises to the top of New as Came back. A push, inside 9 am – 9 pm.
Quiet for 7 days
Going cold. If the firm's idle sweep is on, it moves to the next person on the rota and you are told.
Never on its own
Reject · Deal Closed · change Interested or Site Visit · invent an outcome for a call.
