# Part 1: For the clients

## Sending homes, and the firm's catalogue

- **Send homes from a lead's shortlist, or with no recipient.** One link per
  send, open 7, 15 or 30 days (the firm chooses). Prices show only when the
  sender ticks it. The buyer opens a page of those homes with photos.
- **I'm interested** opens WhatsApp to the agent who holds that buyer, the
  home named. The agent gets "Rahul is interested · <home>".
- **An expired or turned-off link** shows one page with **Talk to us**, and the
  agent is told. From that alert, **Send a fresh link** is one tap.
- **My links** (under Me): each link you sent, how often it was opened, on how
  many devices, and Turn off.
- **A tap from an unknown person** becomes a lead with **Add as lead**; the
  ref in the WhatsApp message opens it on the agent's phone.
- **The public catalogue**: the owner ticks homes into it (in bulk); it can be
  embedded in the firm's website. Its enquiry form makes a lead, source
  Catalogue; a repeat number never makes a second one.
- **Photo links sent before this release stop working**, unless that home is
  in the catalogue (Om, 20 Oct). The catalogue starts empty.
- Agents need a **mobile number on their profile** to send; they are asked
  once.
- **Call me back, Book a visit, New homes like these** on every link and the
  catalogue. Each arrival is one alert to whoever holds the buyer; asking again
  the same day reads "(3×)". A tap never changes an existing lead's booking —
  the agent confirms, and replaces a booked visit only by choosing to. The
  forms and the catalogue share 100 a day per firm.
- A lead with no source reads **Not stated** (it was counted as Website).

## Who gets work

- **Work goes only to people who can take it.** A suspended person, one with no
  sign-in, or one who left is skipped by the rota and offered by no picker;
  Settings → Assigning greys them out with Remove.
- **"Leave unassigned" now does what it says** — it was saved but never read.
  Neither paying firm uses it for leads (read 20 Oct), so nothing changes for
  them.
- An owner adding a lead follows the firm's rule; choosing Unassigned leaves it
  unassigned.

## Fixes people will notice

- The first lead opened after starting the app loads at once (it could spin
  for 20 seconds).
- Reopening a lead from the list could show a shorter shortlist, and removing
  a home then dropped the others. Measured on production: never happened.
- A list's search survives opening a record and coming back.
- History reads "Taken off Kavish", never "→ null" or "via API".
- **Phone numbers carry their country.** Every number is stored
  in full (+91…, +971…); a UK and an Indian number that end alike are two
  people. One phone box with a country picker everywhere.

## Imports

- **Undo an import** removes only what nobody has touched since: a record that
  was called, edited, shortlisted, sent or merged is kept, and History says
  "Undone · N kept". An import the server lost part-way can be undone too.
- No agent can delete a whole import batch any more (any signed-in agent could,
  with no record of it). Read 21 Oct: it was never used at either firm; every
  batch still holds what it added (bhumi 1,271 owners, mahalaxmi 3,731).

## Sign-in

- Sign-in by code is retired (no screen used it; it was open with no limit).
- Sign-in and password reset are limited per address; a reset sends one live
  link, at most one email per 10 minutes.

# Part 2: For us

## What the API deploy does to the database

On its first start (`initSchema`), automatically, in addition to `2026-10-13.md`:

- **Creates** `crm_share_links`, `crm_share_events`, `crm_share_devices`,
  `crm_share_staff_devices`.
- **Adds** `crm_properties.in_catalogue`, `catalogue_slug`;
  `crm_share_events.view_day`, `seen_at` and a unique index (runOnce
  `share_events_one_view_per_day`; the table is new, so nothing conflicts).
- **Settings**: a `sharing` block per firm, defaults only.
- **Writes, once** (`phones_e164`): converts stored phones to E.164.
  **bhumi and mahalaxmi included.** Dry run on production, read-only, 20 Oct:

  | | users | leads | owners | property owner phones | left as they are |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | bhumi | 1 | 4 | 52 | 55 | 11 |
  | mahalaxmi | 3 | 1 | 6 | 7 | 14 |

  Re-checked with the final reader on 21 Oct: unchanged. Placeholder numbers
  (1234567890, 0000000000, straight runs) are stored but never matched.

  Re-run the dry run the morning of the deploy:
  `npx tsx --env-file=.env backend/src/scripts/phone-dry-run.ts --env=production --tenant=bhumi`
  (and `--tenant=mahalaxmi`). The boot log names any row it left.
- A monthly job deletes link views older than 180 days. The tables are new
  in this release, so its first run deletes nothing (read 21 Oct).
- **Creates** `crm_buyer_asks` and `crm_public_form_days` (the daily count).
  **Drops** the `'Website'` default on `crm_leads.source`; existing rows are
  untouched.
- **Adds** `crm_import_jobs.reverted_kept`. At each start, an import still
  `running` after an hour is marked stopped (production has none, 21 Oct).

**Dependency:** `libphonenumber-js` 1.13.14 — `npm ci` on the box.

**Environment:** nothing new is needed. `CRM_NO_SWEEPS` is for test servers
only and is ignored when `APP_ENV=production`; never set it on the box.
