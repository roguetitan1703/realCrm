# Release notes: 28 September 2026

What goes out when `development` is merged into `main` and deployed: 47 commits
since the live `main` (`a3b51c0`). Part 1 is for the clients: what to tell
them and what to show. Part 2 is for us: how to deploy it and what to check.

# Part 1: For the clients

## Calling

- **The call history in their sheets now comes in with the import.** A
  Status, Call Status or Feedback column is read and turned into a status:
  "not intrested" becomes Not Interested, "wrong no" becomes Do Not Call,
  "intrested" becomes Interested, "cnc" or "busy" becomes Contacted. Words it
  doesn't recognise stay on the record as a note. The review step shows the
  split before anything is saved. For Mahalaxmi this matters most: 281 people
  who had already said no were about to be called again.
- **A calling list doesn't need flat numbers.** Only the phone number is
  required. Names and numbers in a project, with the flats not known yet,
  import fine.
- **Tabs: All · Callbacks · New · Contacted · Unassigned.** A **Status**
  dropdown covers every status, including Interested, Key Received, Not
  Interested and Do Not Call, and an **Agent** dropdown covers whose list.
  Inside a project, every number counts that project only.
- **Grouped by project** is where Calling opens, on the phone too. Tap a
  project for its list, and tap × to go back to the projects.
- **Leads or owners with nobody on them are said on the page itself.** Above
  the Leads and Calling lists: "39 owners have nobody on them", with **Show**
  and **Hand out**. Hand out asks first and names who takes turns. It was
  only in Settings before.
- **Configuration filter** (1 BHK, 2 BHK, Shop …) on the calling list, and
  inside a project beside Tower.
- **Callbacks show their date and time** ("Thu, 24 Sept, 9:30 pm"), in red once
  that time has passed. No more "2d late".
- **One project, however it was typed.** "Sai Heights", "sai heights" and
  "SaiHeights" are one project with one Assign button.
- **Key Received → Convert to property** opens the property form already
  filled from the calling row.

## Properties

- **Tabs by what the flat is doing:** All · Available for sale · Available for
  rent · Blocked · Sold · Leased.
- **Grouped by project first.** Tapping a project shows its flats with the
  same search, filters and tabs, and **Add unit** adds a flat to that project.
- **Duplicate:**
  - **One flat:** opens the form filled in, to add its own flat number and
    photos.
  - **Several flats:** tick them and press Duplicate. The copies are marked
    **"No flat no. yet"** until someone fills the number in.
  - **Filter → Flat no. → Not added yet** finds every flat still waiting for
    its number.
- **Photos on the listing itself:** drag to reorder, **Make cover**, and
  remove (it asks first, and Undo brings the photo back).
- **Photo link:** one tap copies a link that opens the photos in a clean
  viewer with the firm's name. It never shows the price. It can be switched
  off, or replaced by a new link.
- **Videos start playing straight away**, in the CRM and on the photo link.
  Before, the whole video had to download first.
- **Verified** badge for a flat someone has visited and checked, with a filter
  for it.
- **The listing keeps its history**: how it was added, every status, price,
  owner and photo change, and who did it.
- **Tower filter** inside a project.

## Deals and contacts

- **Close the deal** records the agreement, rent or sale, with the amount,
  dates and document, and marks the flat Leased or Sold. A rent renews into a
  new agreement, and the agent gets an alert 30 days before it ends.
- **Contacts has three tabs:**
  - **Owners:** Landlords · Sellers. A new **Their flats** column shows
    whether each owner's flat is available, leased or sold.
  - **Tenants:** All · Ending in 30 days.
  - **Buyers.**
- **Find a contact by Project or Locality** on every tab.
- **Call or WhatsApp from the row,** for tenants and buyers too. The call is
  saved in the history.

## Today and Performance

- **Today** on phone and desk is one page with two tabs:
  - **To do:** who to call next.
  - **My work:** what I did today and what I still owe.
- **Performance** shows the owner the team's day in order:
  - people contacted, then how the calls went (picked up, didn't pick up,
    wrong number, replied on WhatsApp), then what came of it;
  - one row per agent, each opening that agent's day;
  - **Needs attention**, naming who each thing sits with.
- **Listings added** counts as an agent's work, on the calling side. Flats
  added by hand, copied or converted all count; imports don't.
- **WhatsApp** counts as contacting someone, the same as a call.
- **Pick any day from a calendar** on Performance. Opening an agent from a
  past day opens them on that day, and Back keeps it.
- **Team today** on the dashboard: one line per person.

## Settings and new firms

- A firm can rename **Deal Closed** (for example to "Booked") and name its
  calling final stage.
- New team members sign in with their **first name** and the password
  **firstname@123**, changed on first sign-in.
- A new firm can add its **logo** during setup.

## Everywhere

- **Search opens with Ctrl+K** (⌘K on a Mac), or "/" when you're not typing.

## Phone

- **Calendar** is in **Me**.
- Calling and Properties open **grouped by project**.
- An owner or manager's phone shows the **firm's** calling list. It showed
  nothing before.

## Fixed (things they may have run into)

- A listing saved without a flat number was stored as flat **A-101**. Unknown
  now stays blank and shows as "No flat no. yet".
- A sheet of listings imported without a Sale/Rent or Locality column was
  saved as "for sale in Pune". What the sheet doesn't say now stays blank.
- In Settings → Assigning, the number beside each name under Calling was
  their leads, not their calling rows.
- The photo link could be "turned on" for a listing with no photos.
- A call made from Contacts was saved nowhere. It is saved on the owner now.
- Being refused an action signed people out. Now it says why.
- Saving the Assigning list briefly showed the old list.
- The screens used system words and dashes. They now read as plain sentences,
  and Settings' tabs are **Stages** and **Assigning**.
- The toast messages at the bottom now wear the firm's own colour.
- On an Android phone with both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business, WhatsApp
  always opened the personal app. It now asks: **WhatsApp** or **Business**.

## A 10-minute demo

1. **Import** a calling sheet with a Call Status column: point out the "18
   Contacted · 4 Do Not Call …" line before confirming.
2. **Calling** opens grouped by project: open one, show the tabs and the
   Status dropdown, Callbacks with their dates, then × back.
3. **Properties** grouped by project: open one, tick two flats, **Duplicate**,
   show the "No flat no. yet" copies and the Flat no. filter.
4. Open a listing: **Make cover**, drag a photo, **Copy photo link** and open
   it on a phone.
5. A Key Received owner: **Convert to property**. A lead: **Close the deal**
   as a rent. Then show it under **Contacts → Tenants**.
6. **Performance**: the team's day, open one agent, and point out Listings
   added.
7. On a phone: Today (To do / My work), Calling grouped, **Me → Calendar**.

# Part 2: For us (not for clients)

## Deploy

**Order: API first, then the frontend.** The new screens call routes the live
API doesn't have: `/owners/tabs`, `/properties/tabs`, `/agreements`,
`/public/gallery`, and the filter options now returned with the contacts and
agreement lists.

1. Merge `development` → `main`.
2. **API, EC2:** `scripts/deploy-api.sh` on the box. It refuses a dirty tree or
   any branch but `main`. Check `/health` shows the new commit.
3. **Frontend, Vercel:** deploy `main` by hand (branch tracking is off). Right
   now Vercel production runs `25f8ae8`, a preview promoted by hand.

What the API deploy does to production, once (all additive):
- **New tables and columns:**
  - `crm_agreements` and its columns;
  - four property columns: `verified_at`, `verified_by`, `gallery_off`,
    `gallery_version`;
  - `sessions.support_by`;
  - `superadmins.failed_logins` and `locked_until`;
  - `audit_log.chain` and its index;
  - the `audit_checks` table, with its `reason` column.
- **New index:** `idx_crm_timeline_day`.
- **One-time step** `2026_09_25_intro_message_no_dashes`: a saved intro message
  loses its dash (approved for all firms).
- **One-time step** `2026_09_25_agreement_party_copy`: fills an agreement's own
  name and phone from its lead. Production had one agreement (urban, a demo),
  already set.

## After deploying

- **Run the owner link:** `npm run link:owners -- --env=production` (a report
  first, then `--apply`). It joins listing owners to calling rows.
- **The superadmin console signs out once:** its old token has no session.
- **The first ledger check reads the whole legacy chain once** (about 8,000
  rows, in pages). After that it reads only new rows.
- **Check on production, read only, with the user's OK:**
  - A-101 is settled: 0 on production in every firm (counted 28 Sep).
  - That Mahalaxmi's Calling opens grouped and the tab counts add up inside a
    project.
- **The audit ledger reads clean after this deploy.** On production it
  reported 31 listings as "changed". That was a reading error in the checker,
  not an edit; with the fix all 10,809 entries verify (checked read only,
  30 Sep). Nothing on production needs re-sealing.
- **Bhumi has 39 calling rows with nobody on them** (their Calling side is set
  to leave rows unassigned). The new bar on Calling will show it; tell them.
- **One Bhumi listing** is marked for sale at under ₹2 lakh. It may be a
  rental saved as a sale; worth asking them.
