# Usage snapshot: bhumi and mahalaxmi, 17 Oct 2026

Read-only, against production (`default_transaction_read_only`, one connection, one scratch script, now deleted).

## Windows and the rules behind every number

- All days are firm days in **IST**.
- **W1 = last 30 days = 17 Sep – 16 Oct.** **W0 = the 30 days before = 18 Aug – 16 Sep.** 17 Oct (today, a partial day) is excluded.
- **Weeks** are 7-day buckets ending 16 Oct, labelled by their first day: 15 Aug, 22 Aug … 10 Oct. That makes nine weeks, 15 Aug – 16 Oct.
- **mahalaxmi was provisioned on 18 Sep.** Its first lead arrived on 21 Sep. It has no W0, and its W1 covers 26 calendar days.
- **Person-authored work** means a `crm_timeline_events` row whose author is not `System` or `Import`, with `metadata.auto` not set to true. This is the same `BY_PERSON` rule that `activityReport.ts` uses.
- **Delpat support is excluded.** `audit_log` holds 7 `support.open` rows: 4 "to make changes" and 3 read only. Any owner-account row written in the 2 hours after a `support.open` counts as Delpat's work, not the firm's. Excluded: bhumi, 57 assignments and 1 WhatsApp; mahalaxmi, 2 agreement events and 3 owner status changes.
  - Support logging only started on 3 Oct. Owner-account setup work before then cannot be told apart from the firm's own work. Examples: mahalaxmi's 11 owner import jobs on 24 Sep, and the integration setup.
- **A call or WhatsApp is a tap in the app.** It does not prove the phone rang or the message was sent. This is the rule Performance already uses.
- **"Lead side" vs "owner side":** a timeline row belongs to a lead if its `record_id` starts with `l_`, and to the owner calling queue if it starts with `own`.
